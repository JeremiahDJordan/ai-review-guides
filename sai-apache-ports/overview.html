Overview: Apache SAI fixes on DataStax main-5.0

An audit compared every Apache cassandra-5.0 and trunk SAI commit with DataStax main-5.0 by behaviour, and filed every bug it found. A series of 107 commits restores the missing Apache fixes that apply to main-5.0 and fixes DataStax bugs in the same code. The review guide says how to review it; the audit report has the details.

Restored by the series: 34 Already present or equivalent: 120 Not applicable: 57 Documented deviation: 4 Deferred to a 6.0-based line: 5
